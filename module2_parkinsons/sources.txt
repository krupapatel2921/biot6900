BIOT 6900 — Module 2 Assignment 2 — Parkinson's Disease
Data sources and decisions log

======================================================================
DISEASE CHOICE
======================================================================
Disease: Parkinson's disease (PD)
Tissue focus: substantia nigra (the brain region where dopaminergic neurons are lost in PD)
Integration level: gene-level (the three layers come from different cohorts; no shared samples)

Gated option considered but not used:
  AMP-PD Knowledge Platform (amp-pd.org) - matched WGS, RNA, and proteomics, but requires a
  data-use agreement (application). Not feasible within the assignment timeline, so the open
  route (GWAS Catalog + GEO + PRIDE/published supplement) was used instead.

======================================================================
LAYER 1: GENOMICS
======================================================================
Source:      NHGRI-EBI GWAS Catalog
Trait:       Parkinson disease (MONDO_0005180)
Link:        https://www.ebi.ac.uk/gwas/efotraits/MONDO_0005180
Access:      Open
Raw file:    data/raw/pd_gwas_association.tsv
Clean file:  data/pd_gwas.tsv

Raw associations:        905
Kept:                    698 (MAPPED_TRAIT exactly "Parkinson disease")
Removed:                 207 rows from other traits:
  - drug-induced dyskinesia / response to levodopa (treatment response, not disease risk)
  - disease progression, survival time, mortality (course of disease, not risk)
  - age at onset, age at diagnosis (timing, not risk)
  - grey matter volume, Lewy body attribute (imaging/pathology phenotypes)
  - dementia in PD, cognitive impairment, cognitive decline (non-motor sub-phenotypes)
  - impulse control disorder, REM sleep behavior disorder (related but separate phenotypes)
  - pesticide exposure, drug use measurement (environmental interaction traits)
Reasoning: only associations with PD risk itself answer "which genes are genetically linked
  to getting PD." Sub-traits measure different biological questions and would mix phenotypes.

Processing:
  - MAPPED_GENE entries with multiple genes were split into one row per gene
  - Per gene, kept the strongest association (maximum -log10 p, from PVALUE_MLOG)
Gene-level result:       393 genes
Sanity check passed:     SNCA, LRRK2, GBA1, TMEM175, MAPT, BST1, RAB29, GAK all present
Note: some mapped genes are pseudogenes or lncRNAs (e.g., HMGN2P18, LINC02210); these did
  not survive the three-way join.

======================================================================
LAYER 2: TRANSCRIPTOMICS
======================================================================
Source:      Gene Expression Omnibus, GSE7621 (Lesnick et al. 2007)
Tissue:      postmortem substantia nigra, PD vs control
Platform:    Affymetrix microarray (analyzed with GEO2R)
Link:        https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE7621
Access:      Open
Raw file:    data/raw/gse7621_geo2r.tsv
Clean file:  data/pd_rna.tsv
Samples:     16 PD, 9 controls (25 total); platform GPL570 (Affymetrix HG-U133 Plus 2.0)

Raw probes:              54,318
Dropped:                 2,197 probes mapping to multiple genes ("///"), because they cannot
                         be assigned to one gene unambiguously
Probe-to-gene collapse:  kept the most significant probe (lowest p-value) per gene
Gene-level result:       20,799 genes

Direction check and sign flip:
  Dopaminergic neuron markers were positive in the GEO2R output
  (SLC6A3 +2.15, DDC +1.94, TH +2.96). These must be DOWN in PD substantia nigra because
  dopaminergic neurons are lost, so the GEO2R contrast was control vs PD.
  Action: log2FC sign flipped so all values are PD vs control.

======================================================================
LAYER 3: PROTEOMICS
======================================================================
Source:      Licker V. et al. (2014) "Proteomic analysis of human substantia nigra identifies
             novel candidates involved in Parkinson's disease pathogenesis."
            Proteomics 14(6):784-794. PMID 24449343. DOI: 10.1002/pmic.201300342
Repository:  ProteomeXchange / PRIDE PXD000427
Data used:   Supplementary tables file (pmic7662-sup-0002-Tables.docx), section
             "All Proteins Relative Expression X / Y"
Access:      Open
Samples:     5 PD cases, 8 controls (postmortem substantia nigra)
Method:      shotgun proteomics with isobaric tag quantification (isobar R package)
Raw file:    data/raw/licker2014_supp.csv
Clean file:  data/pd_protein.tsv

Why the supplement and not raw PRIDE files: PRIDE holds raw mass-spec files that would need
  full reprocessing; the supplement gives per-protein ratios already computed by the authors.

Extraction:
  - The supplement is a PDF-to-Word conversion with rows split across text boxes, so the
    table was extracted programmatically and cross-checked (row-level ID/ratio pairs matched)
  - Paper reports 1,795 quantified proteins; 1,783 extracted (12 lost in document conversion)
  - Gene-level result: 1,779 genes (entries without a gene symbol removed)
  - Significant proteins (flagged * in the supplement): 204, matching the paper's count

Limitations of this layer:
  - NO numeric p-values in the supplement, only a significance flag; the "significant"
    column is kept instead. The multi-evidence score uses |log2FC| magnitude, so p-values
    are not needed for scoring.
  - Direction inferred: the report compares channel groups X/Y without labeling which is PD.
    Set as PD/control because TH is down (0.67) and FTL (1.57), GFAP (1.63), and NEBL (1.86)
    are up, consistent with the paper's validated findings.
  - Proteomics depth (~1,800 proteins) is much smaller than RNA (~20,800 genes), so some key
    PD genes (e.g., LRRK2, GBA1) are not in this layer.

======================================================================
INTEGRATION DECISIONS
======================================================================
Join key:                gene symbol (uppercase, whitespace stripped)
RNA and Protein overlap: 1,684 genes
All three layers:        34 genes
Join type:               inner join (three-way overlap was above the 30-gene threshold,
                         so no GWAS fallback was needed)
Concordance:             RNA vs protein direction agreement; 16 of 34 concordant
Scoring:                 |log2FC| (RNA), |log2FC| (protein), -log10 p (GWAS), each min-max
                         normalized, combined with EQUAL_WEIGHTS (1/3 each), the course default
Output:                  targets_parkinsons.csv (top 15)
